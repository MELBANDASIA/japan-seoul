Still to sort

Every night is booked. One train left: Spacia Kinugawa from Shinjuku on 6 Dec.

Klook still shows the three Shinkansen and Tobu tickets as "confirming", so check they turn to confirmed. Agoda payments come out on 25 Nov (Book & Bed), 27 Nov (Matsuya), 4 Dec (Kyoto) and 10 Dec (Hanok Hotel DAAM). Check GOYOO's charge date in Agoda.

Entry

Australians don't need a visa for either country on this trip. Two free online forms make arrival quicker. Checked on 11 October 2026, so re-check the week before.

  1. Before 1 Dec

    Visit Japan Web

    Free. Up to 90 days visa-free. Register your passport and flight, fill in the immigration and customs forms, and screenshot the QR codes for Narita. Paper forms on arrival still work if you skip it.

    Open Visit Japan Web

  2. 12 to 14 Dec

    Korea e-Arrival card

    Free. Required for foreign visitors. Submit it online in the three days before landing. Do it in Kyoto, not at Haneda at midnight.

    Fill in the e-Arrival card

  3. Nothing to do

    K-ETA

    Not needed. Australians are exempt from K-ETA until 31 December 2026, and you leave Seoul on 28 December. Skip any site that tries to sell you one.

    Official K-ETA site

Stay to stay

Every move from the moment you land in Tokyo to the flight home, step by step. Google Maps in Japan and Naver Map in Korea will show live platforms and times on the day.

Japan

  1. Tue 1 Dec

    Narita Airport T3 → to Book & Bed Tokyo Shinjuku

    About 2 hrsAbout ¥3,250

    1. From Terminal 3, follow the signs to Terminal 2 for the trains: about a 10 to 15 minute walk, or the free shuttle bus.
    2. Book Narita Express (N'EX) to Shinjuku, about 80 to 90 minutes, no changes. Swap your Klook voucher at the JR ticket office first.
    3. At Shinjuku take the East Exit and walk about 10 minutes into Kabukicho.
    4. Book & Bed is on the 8th floor of the Kabukicho APM Building. Check-in from 4 pm.

    Too tired to drag bags? The airport limousine bus goes straight to Shinjuku, then take a GO taxi for the last bit.

  2. Sun 6 Dec

    Book & Bed Tokyo Shinjuku → to Hana no Yado Matsuya, Kinugawa Onsen

    About 2.5 hrsAbout A$40 train

    1. Check out by 11 am and walk about 10 minutes back to Shinjuku Station.
    2. Book Spacia Kinugawa from JR Shinjuku straight to Kinugawa Onsen, about 2 hours, no changes. Reserved seat.
    3. From Kinugawa Onsen station take a short taxi from the rank outside to the ryokan.
    4. Check-in from 3 pm. Matsuya is room only, so buy snacks at the station.

    Sending your big suitcase ahead with Yamato means you only carry a small bag on this day.

  3. Wed 9 Dec

    Hana no Yado Matsuya → to Continue Nijojo Kita, Kyoto

    About 7.5 hrs with bufferTrains paid

    1. Ask the ryokan the night before to book a taxi to Kinugawa Onsen station for about 6:15 am.
    2. 6:41 am Kinu 110 to Asakusa (paid), arriving 8:49.
    3. Ginza Line from Asakusa to Nihonbashi, then a short walk to Tokyo Station, about 20 minutes. Suica.
    4. 11:00 am Nozomi 255 to Kyoto (paid), arriving 1:15 pm. You have about 2 hours of buffer for breakfast.
    5. At Kyoto Station take a taxi from the rank, about 15 to 20 minutes to the hotel, or JR Sagano line to Nijo and a 14-minute walk.
    6. Check-in from 3 pm.

    With bags after a long travel day, the taxi from Kyoto Station is worth it.

  4. Mon 14 Dec

    Continue Nijojo Kita → to 9h nine hours Hamamatsucho, Tokyo

    About 3.5 hrsTrain paid

    1. Book a GO taxi for 6:15 am to Kyoto Station, about 15 minutes. Check out by 10 am, so leave the key as the hotel asks.
    2. 7:06 am Nozomi 70 (paid). Get off at Shinagawa, about 9:05, one stop before Tokyo.
    3. JR Yamanote or Keihin-Tohoku line, two stops to Hamamatsucho, about 6 minutes. Suica.
    4. Short walk to Nine Hours Hamamatsucho. Leave bags, check in from 3 pm.

    If you miss Shinagawa, stay on to Tokyo Station and come back 3 stops on the Yamanote line.

  5. Mon 14 Dec, night

    9h nine hours Hamamatsucho → to Haneda Airport T3, flight MM-809 at 1:55 am

    About 30 minAbout ¥500, Suica

    1. Shower and pack, then walk to Hamamatsucho Station.
    2. Tokyo Monorail to Haneda Airport Terminal 3, about 15 minutes.
    3. Be at the airport by about 11 pm. Last monorails run close to midnight.

    Missed the monorail? A taxi from Hamamatsucho to Haneda is short.

Korea

  1. Tue 15 Dec

    Incheon Airport T1, landing 4:40 am → to Hanok Hotel DAAM, Jongno

    About 1.5 hrsAbout ₩5,000 plus card

    1. After immigration, buy a T-money or WOWPASS card at the convenience store in arrivals and top it up.
    2. Take the AREX all-stop train to Seoul Station, about 60 minutes. The first trains leave from about 5:20 am.
    3. At Seoul Station take subway Line 1 to Jongno 3-ga, then a 9-minute walk, or a short taxi.
    4. Hanok Hotel DAAM is at 19 Yulgok-ro 6-gil, 6 minutes from Anguk station. Check-in from 3 pm, so drop your bags.

    Exhausted after the overnight? A Kakao T taxi from the airport straight to the hotel takes about an hour.

  2. Mon 21 Dec

    Hanok Hotel DAAM → to GOYOO INN & CABINS, Gangnam

    About 45 minSubway on T-money

    1. Check out by 11 am and walk to Anguk station, about 6 minutes.
    2. Subway Line 3 south to Gyodae, about 25 minutes.
    3. Change to Line 2 for Samseong, 4 stops.
    4. Walk to GOYOO on Teheran-ro 77-gil. Check-in from 4 pm, so leave bags first.

    With luggage, a Kakao T taxi is about 30 minutes door to door.

  3. Mon 28 Dec

    GOYOO INN & CABINS → to Incheon Airport T1, flight AK621 at 7:35 am

    About 1.5 hrsBus fare on the day

    1. Alarm for 3:30 am. Check out and walk to the COEX City Airport Terminal bus stop, about 10 minutes.
    2. 4:10 am first 6103 airport bus to Incheon Terminal 1, about 65 minutes.
    3. AirAsia check-in at Terminal 1. Flight via Kuala Lumpur, landing in Perth at 3:40 am on 29 Dec.

    Backup: book a Kakao T taxi for about 4:15 am the night before.

Getting around

The eSIM covers Japan and Korea, so maps work from the moment you land.

  • Suica

    JapanTrains, buses, konbini

    Add it to Apple Wallet before you fly and top up with your card. It works on every metro and local train in this plan.

  • Haneda at midnight

    14 DecTerminal 3

    Trains stop around midnight, so take one that gets you in by about 11 pm. Terminal 3 stays open overnight.

  • Naver Map and T-money

    KoreaDownload before Seoul

    Google Maps can't do walking directions in Korea, so use Naver. Buy a T-money card at an Incheon convenience store for the subway and buses. WOWPASS does the same and doubles as a payment card, but its transit and shopping balances top up separately. Also get Papago for translating and Kakao T for taxis.

  • Incheon, both ways

    15 Dec arrival, 28 Dec departure

    You land before the first AREX train, so wait in arrivals, then take the AREX to Seoul Station and the subway to Jongno. For the 7:35 am flight home, the first 6103 bus leaves COEX at 4:10 am and takes about 65 minutes.

  • Sending the suitcase ahead

    OptionalAbout a day in transit

    Yamato can send your case from Book & Bed to the Kyoto hotel, so you only take a small bag to the ryokan. Ask at reception the day before.
