Entry

Australians don't need a visa for either country on this trip. Two free online forms make arrival quicker. Checked on 11 October 2026, so re-check the week before.

  1. Before 1 Dec

    Visit Japan Web

    Free. Up to 90 days visa-free. Register your passport and flight, fill in the immigration and customs forms, and screenshot the QR codes for Narita. Paper forms on arrival still work if you skip it.

    Open Visit Japan Web

  2. 12 to 14 Dec

    Korea e-Arrival card

    Free. Required for foreign visitors. Submit it online in the three days before landing. Do it in Kyoto, not at Haneda at midnight.

    Fill in the e-Arrival card

  3. Nothing to do

    K-ETA

    Not needed. Australians are exempt from K-ETA until 31 December 2026, and you leave Seoul on 28 December. Skip any site that tries to sell you one.

    Official K-ETA site

Things to do

A short list for each place. Most temples and shrines take cash only.

Tokyo

  • 浅草

    Senso-ji

    Free

    AsakusaGo before 8 am

    Tokyo's oldest temple, with the Nakamise snack street leading up to it.

  • 都庁

    Metropolitan Government Building

    Free

    ShinjukuClear days you can see Fuji

    A 45th-floor view over the city, ten minutes' walk from Book & Bed.

  • 明治

    Meiji Jingu

    Free

    HarajukuMorning

    A shrine in a forest right next to Takeshita Street.

  • 横丁

    Omoide Yokocho

    ShinjukuEvening

    Tiny lantern-lit yakitori stalls by Shinjuku Station. Most seat about eight people.

Kinugawa and Nikko

  • 東照宮

    Nikko Tosho-gu

    NikkoHalf a day

    A heavily carved, gold-leafed shrine complex in the cedar forest. Home of the three wise monkeys.

  • 憾満

    Kanmangafuchi Abyss

    Free

    Nikko20 min walk from Shinkyo

    A riverside path lined with about 70 stone Jizo statues in red caps.

  • 華厳

    Kegon Falls

    Lake ChuzenjiBus up the mountain

    A 97 m waterfall. It can be icy in December, so check the weather first.

Kyoto and Kanazawa

  • 伏見

    Fushimi Inari

    Free

    South KyotoDawn

    Thousands of orange torii up the mountain. Crowds thin out past the halfway point.

  • 嵐山

    Arashiyama bamboo grove

    Free

    West KyotoBefore 8 am

    Pair it with Tenryu-ji's garden next door.

  • 兼六園

    Kenroku-en

    KanazawaDay trip, 11 Dec

    One of Japan's three great gardens. In December the pines are tied with rope cones against the snow.

  • 近江町

    Omicho Market

    KanazawaLunch

    Kaisendon seafood bowls. Winter is crab season on this coast.

Seoul · Hongdae & Yeonnam-dong

  • 홍대

    Hongdae street performances

    Free

    HongdaeEvenings, busiest on weekends

    K-pop dance crews and buskers on the main walking street.

  • 연남

    Yeonnam-dong and Gyeongui Line Forest Park

    Free

    Yeonnam-dongAfternoon

    A long park on old rail tracks, lined with cafés and small shops.

  • 망원

    Mangwon Market

    MangwonLunch

    Cheap street food like hotteok, tteokbokki and fried chicken. The Han River park is a short walk away.

  • 이화

    Ewha Womans University

    Free

    SinchonMorning

    A striking sunken campus building, with cheap clothing shops on the streets around it.

Seoul · Jongno & Bukchon

  • 경복궁

    Gyeongbokgung in a hanbok

    Free entry in hanbok

    JongnoMorning

    Rent a hanbok from a shop by the gate. Closed Tuesdays.

  • 익선동

    Ikseon-dong

    JongnoEvening

    Narrow hanok alleys turned into cafés, tea houses and bars.

  • 광장

    Gwangjang Market

    JongnoLunch

    Bindaetteok, mayak gimbap and knife noodles at the stalls.

  • 남산

    Namsan Seoul Tower

    NamsanSunset or night

    Cable car up, love-lock fences and the best night view of the city. Pair it with Myeongdong.

Seoul · Gangnam and day trips

  • 별마당

    Starfield Library

    Free

    COEX, GangnamNear GOYOO

    Two-storey bookshelves inside the COEX mall. It usually has a big Christmas display.

  • 롯데

    Lotte World and Seoul Sky

    JamsilChristmas Eve

    An indoor theme park with an ice rink, and the observatory in Lotte World Tower next door.

  • 성수

    Seongsu

    East SeoulAll day

    Converted factories, concept cafés and brand pop-ups. Good for content.

  • 남이섬

    Nami Island and Garden of Morning Calm

    GapyeongDay trip, about 1 hr by ITX

    Tree-lined island walks by day and the garden's winter light festival after dark.
